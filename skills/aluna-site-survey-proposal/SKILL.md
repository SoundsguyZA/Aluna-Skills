# Site Survey and Proposal

1. Assign stable opening IDs. Record supplied measurements in millimetres, source/photo, width and height at relevant positions, reveal, mounting surface, obstructions, hardware, operation, proposed stacking/hinging, colour and environment. Keep raw site measurements separate from derived dimensions and assumptions. Flag inconsistent readings.
2. Check selected product and installation guidance against current manufacturer documents or approved technical references. Never invent tolerances, fixing methods, ratings, or prices. If unverified, mark pending and identify the check. Calculate indicative quantities with explicit units and formula; do not treat them as a manufacturing take-off.
3. Produce client-facing proposal with scope, dated technical schedule, before/after concepts, alternatives where suitable, exclusions and unresolved confirmations. Label generated images conceptual; preserve existing architecture and operation. Technical survey and approved specification take precedence over renders. Include prices only from authorised inputs, distinguishing taxes and assumptions.
4. Verify all opening IDs, quantities, dimensions, product configurations and consistency between text and images. Require responsible professional approval for ordering, manufacture or installation; do not claim approval without evidence.

## Acceptance scenarios
- Conflicting heights: flag both and request verification before final manufacturing dimensions.
- Proposed render differs from recorded product: explicitly label the alternative.
- No pricing: label as survey/visual proposal, not final financial quotation.
